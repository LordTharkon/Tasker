Controls
  • Click an empty cell to place
  • Click a piece to select it
  • R / F / T rotates, flips or toggles railings on the selected building (or the one you're placing)
  • O toggles overwrite: clicks replace whatever is underneath
  • Del or right-click deletes
  • Ctrl+Z undoes
  • 1–9 and 0 jump to floors 1–10, PgUp / PgDn step
  • Click the highlighted palette piece again (or Esc) to put it down and just select
  • Esc deselects
  • Upper floors may overhang, but need at least one building cube underneath

How to use

  • Pick a piece in the bar at the bottom, then tap an empty cell to place it
  • Tap a placed piece to select it: Rotate, Flip, Rail and Delete then act on it
  • With nothing selected, Rotate, Flip and Rail set up the next piece you place
  • Select puts down the piece you're holding, so taps only select
  • Overwrite: taps replace whatever is underneath
  • Pinch or use + / − to zoom the plan, drag to scroll big boards
  • Switch to 3D at the top: drag to orbit, pinch to zoom, double-tap to reset
  • Upper floors may overhang, but need at least one building cube underneath
