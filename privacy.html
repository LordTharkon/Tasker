Privacy Policy

Tabletop Terrain Planner · Effective 29 September 2026

In short: Tabletop Terrain Planner does not collect, send or share any personal data. Your layouts stay on your device.

What the app stores

The app saves the following in your browser's local storage on your own device so your work is there next time you open it:

  • Your current terrain layout (placed pieces, floors and table settings)
  • Interface preferences, such as panel sizes and which view is open

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it, and it is not linked to your identity.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics, tracking or advertising
  • No cookies
  • No third-party services or scripts
  • No selling or sharing of data with anyone

Importing and exporting

When you export a layout (JSON or PNG image), the file is created on your device and saved wherever you choose. When you import a layout file, it is read locally in the app and is not uploaded anywhere.

Offline use

The app caches its own files (page, icons) on your device so it can work offline. The cache contains only the app itself, not information about you.

Hosting

Like any website, the server that delivers the app may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) for security and reliability. We do not use these logs to identify or track you.

Deleting your data

You can remove everything the app has stored by clearing site data for this app in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the app on your device.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
